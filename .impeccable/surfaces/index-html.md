## Scope and mode

`index.html` — the whole app: star board, claim-reward prompt, prize wheel, win card, and the "Prizes & kids" editor. Mode: Experience for the board, prompt, wheel and win card (kids watch); Operate rules inside the editor (plain, forgiving controls).

## Audience, job, constraints

- Teacher taps one big star button (or key 1–8) each time a kid answers; must never fumble mid-lesson.
- Kids (ESL, ~4–10) read everything through picture, number, color, motion, sound and the repeated "Good job, NAME!".
- Must survive screen-share compression and a classroom TV; must still celebrate with reduce-motion on (static confetti, praise held still).

## Chosen direction

Classroom craft paper (grounded candidate 5, seed 5412cb1a), chosen by the user over Doodle notebook, Arcade prize corner and the classic kids' app. Replaced the earlier Painted Wooden Toybox, which the user found not kid-like enough.

## Flow

Star → gold foil sticker sticks on the kid's paper strip, "Good job, NAME!" and a confetti pop. Strip full → "Reward time!" card (Claim reward / Later). Claim → paper-plate wheel pops in (it is never on the board otherwise; desktop editor shows a live preview). Spin → wheel steps back, a card hung on yarn says "Good job, NAME!" with the prize; a gift sticker is added next to the kid's name.
